# Brief: Mikrodars taqdimoti · 1-sinf matematika

"1 dan 5 gacha boʻlgan sonlar bilan amallar bajarish": a 1st-grade math micro-lesson, turned from an
11-slide PowerPoint into a step-by-step web presentation on the deck kit's engine, without the map.

## Audience

The commission at the micro-lesson defence ("himoya") and the other course participants
("tinglovchilar") of a teacher-training course in Jizzax. They are teachers and methodologists:
they know the 1st-grade curriculum and judge the method, not the arithmetic.
(Inferred from the speaker notes, slide 4: "Himoyada aynan 12 daqiqalik fragmentni namoyish qilish qulay.")

## The one message

"Oʻyin orqali bola faqat hisoblamaydi — u koʻradi, tanlaydi, izohlaydi va oʻzini tekshiradi." (slide 11)

Emphasis, from the notes of slide 1: arithmetic within 1–5 is consolidated through games, and the
presentation shows practical games more than theory.

## Length, place and screen

- Steps or minutes: the source has 11 slides; the web deck has about 29 steps. The defence shows the
  12-minute fragment of the lesson (notes, slide 4).
- Where it is shown: at the defence, from a web link opened in Chrome (the room needs internet).
  With no map, the deck itself needs no internet once loaded.
- Screen size: 1920 × 1080 assumed (hall projector); checked at 1366 × 768 too.
